# Paper photograph

One photograph of real crumpled paper. The owner turned down drawn paper on
2026-11-24 ("We would need actual paper textures not try to make them"),
picked two photographs from a sheet of ten, and on 2026-11-25 kept only this
one ("this is the only paper i like out of the bunch"). It may be bundled and
passed on.

What was done to it: made grey, 2880 px wide (the whole photograph), and the
uneven lighting taken out (each pixel divided by the broad brightness around
it), so flat paper sits at level 173 of 255, folds are darker and catches of
light brighter. It is a gentle photograph, so its contrast about that level
was raised 3.6 times (after a slight blur, so camera noise is not raised with
it). The script that did it is kept beside this note as
`prepare-paper.js.txt` (it needs `sharp`; not part of the build). The `-small`
file is the same at 480 px, for the tiles. See `../paper.js`.

The second photograph of 2026-11-24 (PxHere 1442969, CC0) was taken out on
2026-11-25 and is no longer in the project.
